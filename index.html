03 RESEARCH

Research in Progress — AI in Cybersecurity

My research journey is ongoing. I am developing a structured workflow to explore cybersecurity specializations, identify meaningful research gaps, and investigate how AI can contribute to cybersecurity through literature analysis, research questions, study design, and evidence-based evaluation.

  1. Phase 01 — Research onboarding and methodology. Learn academic workflows, explore cybersecurity specializations, refine an AI-in-cybersecurity research area, and establish a structured approach to research problems.
  2. Phase 02 — Literature review and comparative analysis. I plan to review studies through Google Scholar, IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, ScienceDirect, SpringerLink, and other relevant databases, comparing methods, datasets, metrics, results, limitations, and conclusions.
  3. Phase 03 — Identify and justify research gaps. Investigate methodological, data, performance, and evaluation gaps. Illustrative example, not a finding: a detector may perform well on a benchmark but need stronger evaluation against changing attack behavior or realistic operational conditions.
  4. Phase 04 — Formulate research questions. Develop scoped, literature-supported, answerable questions with suitable methods and defined evaluation criteria; no final research question is claimed yet.
  5. Phase 05 — Study design and demonstrated contribution. Define methodology, data strategy and ethics, analytics, evaluation and reproducibility, and the study's net contribution.

Alongside this longer-term work, I plan smaller cybersecurity research notes and seminar writeups, beginning with network security and expanding into other domains over time.
