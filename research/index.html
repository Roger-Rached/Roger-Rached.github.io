Research in Progress — AI in Cybersecurity

ONGOING RESEARCH JOURNEY

Cyber Research Group · Middlesex University Dubai

My research journey is ongoing. I am developing a structured workflow to explore cybersecurity specializations, identify meaningful research gaps, and investigate how AI can contribute to cybersecurity. My approach combines literature analysis, clearly formulated research questions, systematic study design, and evidence-based evaluation.

This is a planned methodology, not a completed academic study. No specific findings, completed literature review, or finalized research question are claimed.

AI SecuritySecure AIAI for Cybersecurity
PHASE 01

Research onboarding and methodology

Understand academic research workflows and methodology, explore cybersecurity specializations, select and refine an AI-in-cybersecurity research area, and establish a structured approach to investigating research problems.

PHASE 02

Literature review and comparative analysis

I intend to review Google Scholar, IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, ScienceDirect, SpringerLink, and other relevant academic databases. I will compare study methodologies, datasets, evaluation criteria, results, limitations, and conclusions; this review is not represented as complete.

PHASE 03

Identify and justify research gaps

Investigate methodological weaknesses, missing or biased data, performance shortcomings, and evaluation gaps.

Illustrative example only: an initial review might suggest that a detector performs well on a benchmark but has limited testing against changing attack behavior or realistic operational conditions. That would be a hypothesis to verify against literature, not an established finding.

PHASE 04

Formulate research questions

Use evidenced gaps to develop clearly scoped, answerable questions relevant to AI in cybersecurity. Questions should be supported by the literature, assessable with an appropriate methodology, and connected to defined evaluation criteria. A final research question has not yet been confirmed here.

PHASE 05

Study design and demonstrated contribution

  • Methodology: study design and justification.
  • Data strategy: sources, acquisition, preparation, quality, and ethics.
  • Analytics: analytical methods and evaluation procedures.
  • Evaluation: metrics, baselines, validation, limitations, and reproducibility.
  • Net contribution: the knowledge, evidence, or practical value added.

The intended outcome is a defensible, evidence-based contribution to AI in cybersecurity.
